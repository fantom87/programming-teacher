# Notes from people who looked at this

Scribble anything here — no format, no structure, no need to be polite or
thorough. A one-line "this bit confused me" is genuinely more useful than a
paragraph you had to sit down and compose.

Two ways to leave notes, whichever is less friction:

- **Add to this file** and commit (or open a PR if you'd rather).
- **Open an issue** on the repo — better for anything you want to go
  back and forth on.

If you want somewhere to start: `README.md` explains what the thing is and
points at the parts most worth attacking. `docs/KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md` lists
what's already known to be unfinished, so you don't waste time reporting it.
