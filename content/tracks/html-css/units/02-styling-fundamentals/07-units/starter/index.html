On Keeping a Notebook

The cheapest tool a thinker owns is a notebook that forgives bad handwriting. Mine lives in a coat pocket and has opinions about umbrellas, train schedules, and one particularly good sandwich in October.

The trick is not writing well. The trick is writing at all — five words at a bus stop beats a paragraph you never started. Volume first; the good sentences sneak in on their own.

Reread it once a season. You will meet a slightly younger stranger with excellent ideas they completely forgot about.
