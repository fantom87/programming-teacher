The Case for a Slower Web

Somewhere between the modem screech and the infinite scroll, browsing stopped being a stroll and became a treadmill. This essay is about walking again.

A slow site loads once, says its piece, and lets you leave. It respects the reader the way a good essay respects a Sunday morning: no pop-ups, no pleading, just type on a page and room to think.

The good news is that slowness is a choice, and it is still on the menu.
