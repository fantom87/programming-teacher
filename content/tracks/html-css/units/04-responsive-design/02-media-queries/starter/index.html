Ridgeline Trail Journal

Day 3: the saddle at last

We reached the saddle just before noon, wind combing the grass flat in long silver strokes. From up there the whole valley looks like a rumpled green blanket somebody almost folded.

Lunch was crackers, hard cheese, and the last of the apricots. A marmot supervised from a rock and was paid one apricot for its trouble.

Down by four, boots off by five, asleep before the stars finished showing up.

Trail facts

  • Distance: 9.4 miles
  • Climb: 2,100 ft
  • Water: two creek crossings
  • Marmots bribed: one
