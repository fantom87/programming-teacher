Our story

The Harborlight opened in 1921 as a vaudeville house with a film projector bolted on as an afterthought. The projector won. Converted for sound in 1929, it has missed exactly one week of screenings since — the storm of 1954, and even then the organ played on.

When the six-screen opened inland, everyone said we were finished. A community trust bought the building in 1987, re-raked the floor, and kept the programme stubborn: four films a week, nothing you can stream yet, one screen big enough to matter.

Same beam of light since 1921. We just keep it pointed at good films.

— Rosa Imbert, projectionist since 1979
