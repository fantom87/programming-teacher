Why Line Length Matters

Try reading this paragraph with the window at full width. Your eyes travel a long way to the end of each line, and on the way back they have to find the start of the next one without slipping. The wider the line, the more often you lose your place — which is why every book, newspaper, and well-made website limits how long a line of text can get.

Typographers aim for roughly forty-five to seventy-five characters per line. You do not need to count characters, though. You just need to cap the width of the box the text lives in, and let the browser do the rest. That is exactly what you are about to do to this page.

When you finish, resize the preview. Narrow windows should still work perfectly, and wide ones should show a calm, centered column with quiet space on either side.
