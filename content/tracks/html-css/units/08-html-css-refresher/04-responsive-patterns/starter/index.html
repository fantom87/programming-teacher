Fog on the Ridge Line

We left before dawn, which the guidebook recommends and the body protests. By the second switchback the valley was a bowl of milk, and the ridge floated on it like a rumor.

The summit register held three entries from this week — two terse, one a full paragraph about a thermos left behind. If it is yours, the ranger station has it and, reportedly, opinions about your tea.

Trip facts

  • Climb — 480 m
