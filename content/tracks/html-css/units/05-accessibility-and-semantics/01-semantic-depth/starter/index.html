Community Garden Doubles in Size

After two years on a waiting list, the garden committee has secured the empty lot behind the library. Twelve new plots open this spring.

All twelve beds went up in a single weekend.

Applications open Monday at the library front desk. Bring a watering can and modest expectations for your first tomato summer.
