Sourdough Starter Care Manual

Congratulations on your new dependent. It lives in a jar, eats flour, and will outlast most houseplants if you follow this manual.

Daily Feeding

Once a day, discard half and stir in fresh flour and water. The same time each day keeps the rise predictable.

Choosing a Flour

Any unbleached flour works. Whole wheat wakes a sluggish starter right up.

Water Temperature

Lukewarm. Hot water cooks it; cold water just slows the day down.

Troubleshooting

Every starter has moods. These are the two you will actually meet.

Gray Liquid on Top

That's hooch — the starter is hungry, not dead. Pour it off and feed.

No Bubbles

Move the jar somewhere warmer and give it two days before panicking.
